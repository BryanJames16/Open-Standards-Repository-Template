## 2. Governance Principles

The project aims to follow these principles. Adapt them to fit the project's needs:

- **Openness:** Interested people can understand how to participate and how decisions are made.
- **Transparency:** Decisions and their rationale are recorded and made available, except where a legitimate privacy or safety concern requires otherwise.
- **Fair participation:** Relevant viewpoints are considered, and no single participant should dominate the process.
- **Traceability:** Significant proposals, reviews, and decisions have a record that contributors can refer to later.
- **Proportionate process:** The project uses only the roles, reviews, and approval steps needed for its work.
