# Decision-Making Guide

Use this guide when defining or applying the decision-making approach in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md). **`GOVERNANCE.md` remains the authoritative statement of who can decide and which process the project follows.** Do not maintain a second set of decision rules here.

## Choose a Simple Method

Choose a method that fits the project's size and formality. Common approaches include:

- **Consensus:** Seek broad agreement, consider concerns, and record any unresolved dissent. Consensus does not necessarily mean unanimity.
- **Delegated decision:** A named maintainer, editor, working group, or governing body decides matters within its agreed authority.

A project may use different methods for different kinds of decisions. For example, routine editorial corrections may be delegated, while changes to normative requirements or formal approval of a standard may be reserved to the governing body.

## Voting (Optional)

Use voting only if it suits your project. `GOVERNANCE.md` should state who may vote, the quorum and approval threshold, how ties and abstentions are handled, and where results are recorded. If these rules are not needed, omit voting and use the project's normal decision method.

## Make Decisions Traceable

Record significant decisions in the issue, discussion, or meeting record where the topic was handled. Include only what is needed to understand the outcome:

- What was decided.
- Who or which body made the decision.
- When it was decided.
- The reason for the decision, including how material concerns were addressed.

Use existing project records rather than creating a separate document for every decision. Keep records public where practical, subject to privacy, security, and conduct concerns.

## Handle Concerns

Give relevant participants a reasonable opportunity to raise concerns before a decision is finalized. Explain how unresolved objections, requests for reconsideration, or appeals are handled in `GOVERNANCE.md` if the project needs a formal route.

## Keep the Process Proportionate

Do not require a vote, meeting, or separate decision record for every pull request. Follow the authority and approval rules in `GOVERNANCE.md`, and distinguish repository review from formal approval of a standard.
